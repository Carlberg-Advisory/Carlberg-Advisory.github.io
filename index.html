The challenge

Organisations rarely lack ambition.

They have strategies. Initiatives. Talented people. Technology. Transformation programmes.

Yet many still struggle to translate all that activity into sustained progress.

Decisions take too long to land. Priorities compete. Teams optimise locally. Initiatives start strongly and gradually lose momentum. Delivery becomes dependent on a handful of people who somehow keep everything moving.

The problem is often not effort. It is the system around the effort.

That is the territory I work in.
