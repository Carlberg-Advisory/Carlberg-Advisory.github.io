Advisory

Strategy is useful. Making it work is more interesting.

My work typically sits where commercial growth, technology, operating models and execution intersect.

I work with leadership teams, investors and organisations facing growth, transformation or scaling challenges — particularly where the answer crosses traditional functional boundaries.

Areas of work

Growth & Commercial Excellence

Customer segmentation and ICP design, go-to-market, commercial operating models, sales and operations alignment, B2B commerce and turning capabilities into scalable propositions.

Scalable Operating Models

Productisation, modular operating models, organisational design, standardisation and reducing complexity created by growth.

Technology-Enabled Transformation

Using digital platforms, data, AI, automation and ecosystems to change how the business actually operates — not simply adding technology to existing processes.

Transformation & Interim Leadership

Hands-on support where implementation matters as much as diagnosis: leading defined transformations, establishing operating structures and creating momentum until the organisation can carry it forward itself.
