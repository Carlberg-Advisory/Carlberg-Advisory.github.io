Advisory

Strategy is useful. Making it work is more interesting.

I work where commercial growth, technology, operating models and execution intersect — particularly when the challenge doesn’t fit neatly into one function.

When to call

Usually, it starts with a situation — not a consulting category.

The work often crosses commercial, operational and technology boundaries. These are three situations where that becomes particularly valuable.

01
Growth · Complexity · Scale

When growth has outgrown the operating model.

The business is growing, but complexity, dependencies and bespoke ways of working are growing with it. What worked at one scale no longer stretches far enough.

Typical work
  • Scalable operating models
  • Productisation
  • Organisational design
  • Commercial & operational alignment
02
Transformation · Execution · Momentum

When transformation isn’t translating into results.

The programme exists. The initiatives exist. The steering meetings exist. But progress is difficult to see, priorities keep moving and execution remains inconsistent.

Typical work
  • Directional Delivery Engine
  • Execution structure
  • Leadership alignment
  • Transformation leadership
03
Capability · Proposition · Market

When a capability needs to become a business.

The technology, expertise or operational capability exists — but it hasn’t yet become a proposition customers can understand, buy and scale with.

Typical work
  • Segmentation & ICP
  • Go-to-market
  • Productisation
  • B2B commerce & platform models
